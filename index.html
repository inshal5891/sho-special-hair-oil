✦ The Mindful Hair Ritual

The 4-Step Application Ritual

Recommended 2–3 times per week for optimal scalp balance.

1

Warm in Palms

Dispense 2–4 pumps into dry palms. Rub hands together gently for 5 seconds to activate the botanical terpenes.

2

Gentle Scalp Massage

Part hair into sections. Massage with fingertips in slow circular motions for 3–5 minutes, focusing on temples and crown.

3

Smooth Down Lengths

Draw remaining golden nectar from mid-lengths to ends to lock in hydration and protect fragile split tips.

4

Rest & Cleanse

Leave for 1–2 hours or overnight. Wash out thoroughly with a gentle sulfate-free cleanser. Witness the glow.
